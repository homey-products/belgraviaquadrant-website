The firm

One idea, four corners.

We would rather do fewer things exceptionally well than many things adequately.

Belgravia Quadrant is built around one idea: every corner of a property deal, meaning buyer, seller, agent and lender, looked after properly.

The name joins two ideas. Belgravia is the London district developed from the 1820s for the Grosvenor estate, known for well-planned, well-built property. A quadrant is one quarter of a whole. Together they give the firm its four-part framework: four steps and four corners.

We are traditional in the things that matter: careful drafting, clear advice, proper record keeping and courtesy. We are modern where it helps clients: efficient processes, sensible use of technology and plain communication.

How we are structured

Teams with their own brand. One regulated firm.

Belgravia Quadrant allows specialist teams within the firm to operate under their own brand. Each team takes ownership of the quality of its work and the processes behind it, so clients deal with a focused group that knows its field.

Every team sits inside Belgravia Quadrant's regulated legal framework. The firm, not the brand, is the SRA-authorised body, and the same rules, supervision and client protections apply whichever team you instruct.
